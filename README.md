# ZARtech Digital HQ

Build the complete flagship website for ZARtech Solutions.

This is NOT a generic agency website, NOT a SaaS template, and NOT a conventional IT-company website.

This website is the public digital headquarters of ZARtech Solutions and must itself demonstrate the quality of our design, frontend engineering, product thinking, interaction design, responsive development and technical capability.

The experience should make a serious business owner immediately understand:

ZARtech doesn't just talk about technology. ZARtech designs and builds digital products, business systems and experiences.

The website must feel like a premium digital technology studio / product engineering company / creative technology lab.

The design should be distinctive enough that it could plausibly be featured on Awwwards, CSS Design Awards or a premium digital design showcase.

Do NOT imitate any existing company. Take inspiration from the level of craft found in premium products such as Linear, Vercel, Apple, Stripe and high-end creative technology studios, but create a completely original ZARtech identity.
